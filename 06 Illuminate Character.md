## 06 Illuminate Character

The Point Light Emission feature simulates realistic fire lighting on the character and nearby environment without relying on Lumen or dynamic GI.

It provides controlled fire illumination using a custom Light Function, making it ideal when Lumen is disabled or when separate control over fire lighting is needed without adjusting material self-illumination.
